CHILD SAFETY PRINCIPLES

A safe space to learn and grow.

The product is designed to support school-led learning without exposing children to public social features.

🔐

Private by design

School and class membership controls access. Students do not browse other students or other schools' private courses.

🧑‍🏫

Trusted adults

Schools manage teachers and course access. Guardians only see a child's learning summary after a consented link.

⚑

Speak up

Students can report content concerns. Authorized school reviewers can triage, resolve and record the outcome.

Our default safety rules

  • No public profiles or open child-to-child direct messaging.
  • No advertising, public rankings, shame-based scores or punitive streaks.
  • Course content should be age-appropriate, accessible, accurate and licensed.
  • Schools should review courses before students see them.
  • Collect only the information needed to provide learning and protect accounts.
  • Progress reports are support tools, not automated labels about a child.

For schools and guardians

Use school-managed accounts, keep passwords private, review guardian links carefully, and contact your institution's safeguarding lead for urgent concerns. The platform report form is not an emergency service.

A formal safeguarding policy, named responsible contact, incident response process, staff training, and jurisdiction-specific child-protection procedures are required before live launch.
