PRIVACY OVERVIEW · DRAFT FOR REVIEW

Learning should feel safe.

BrightPath is designed for school-managed learning. This plain-language overview explains the intended privacy approach; it is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific legal privacy notice.

What the platform uses

School-managed usernames, display names, class placement, institution membership, assigned courses, lesson completion, quiz results, guardian links, safety reports and necessary account/security records.

What we avoid by default

Public student profiles, child-to-child direct messaging, public leaderboards, advertising identifiers, third-party behavioral advertising, and unnecessary personal information such as a child's home address or birth date.

Who can see learning information?

Students see their own assigned learning and progress. Teachers see learning information for classes they are authorized to teach. Institution administrators can access records for their institution. Guardians see limited summaries only after their account email is verified, the school creates a link, and the guardian explicitly confirms consent. Platform support access should be limited and audited.

How long is information kept?

The service operator must define retention, account deletion, institution offboarding, exports and backup expiry before launch. Do not use real child data until these policies are approved and implemented.

Questions or concerns

Students and guardians can ask the school for help or report a content/privacy concern from a course. Schools should provide a named safeguarding contact and a process for urgent concerns outside the platform.

Before launch, replace this draft with a formal privacy notice naming the operator, data controller/processor roles, lawful basis, applicable rights, contact details, vendors, retention periods and the requirements for each country served.
